# Replit AI Prompt – "Help Me Make the Right Trade" Engine

## Objective

Build an **AI Decision Engine** that acts like an experienced professional trader. The goal is **not** to tell the user what they must do, but to analyze the available evidence, identify high-probability opportunities, explain the reasoning, quantify risk, and recommend whether the user should consider entering a trade, waiting, or avoiding the market.

The AI should provide transparent, explainable analysis rather than treating its outputs as guarantees.

---

# Core AI Workflow

Whenever a user uploads a chart or selects a market:

1. Analyze the uploaded chart using computer vision.
2. Identify all technical indicators, chart patterns, and market structure.
3. Pull the latest market price.
4. Retrieve recent news related to the asset.
5. Analyze overall market sentiment.
6. Check for upcoming economic events.
7. Evaluate multiple possible trade scenarios.
8. Calculate confidence scores for each scenario.
9. Recommend the highest-probability action.
10. Explain every recommendation in plain English.

---

# AI Trade Decision

The AI must choose only one of the following:

✅ BUY (LONG)

✅ SELL (SHORT)

✅ WAIT

✅ NO TRADE

The AI should never force a trade if the evidence is weak.

# Important Behavior Rules

* Never recommend a trade unless the confidence score meets a configurable threshold (for example, 75/100).
* Prefer **WAIT** or **NO TRADE** over low-quality setups.
* Clearly distinguish facts (e.g., detected chart features) from AI inferences (e.g., likely continuation).
* Explain every recommendation with evidence so users can understand the reasoning.
* Present outputs as educational decision support, not guaranteed outcomes or personalized financial advice.
